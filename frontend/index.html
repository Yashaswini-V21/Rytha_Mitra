What RythaGelathi Delivers

Core decision tools described in the project README, shaped for real field use.

Crop Recommender

Top crop choices based on NPK, weather profile, rainfall patterns, and district context.

Irrigation Guidance

Daily timing and practical watering guidance using forecast and field conditions.

Pest Risk Signals

Temperature and humidity based risk awareness to prepare early interventions.

Fertilizer Optimization

Soil-aware NPK direction to avoid overuse and reduce unnecessary input cost.

Agentic Intelligence Pipeline

RythaGelathi uses coordinated agents to mirror practical advisory workflow.

CropAdvisor

Random Forest + SHAP explanation for transparent crop suitability suggestions.

MarketAnalyst

Price and profitability support through mandi-aligned data interpretation.

WeatherIntel

Weather compatibility checks and risk signaling for near-term planning.

SoilExpert

Nutrient and soil health awareness to reduce mismatch between crop and soil profile.
